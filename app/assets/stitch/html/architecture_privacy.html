Mathematical Foundations: Dimensionless Metric Invariants

Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) Formulation

Computed on 6 key landmarks per ocular aperture ($p_1 \dots p_6$). Invariant to camera distance, scale transforms, and head positioning. As the eyelid descends over the pupil, the vertical Euclidean lengths diminish while the horizontal denominator remains constant.

EAR = ( ||p2 - p6|| + ||p3 - p5|| ) / ( 2 * ||p1 - p4|| )
SAFE BASELINE: > 0.25 WARNING THRESHOLD: 0.20 - 0.25 MICROSLEEP BREACH: < 0.18
LANDMARK CONFIGURATION (TERTIARY GEOMETRY ONLY)
Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) Formulation

Separates yawning events from regular driver speech through temporal duration integration. Speech exhibits rapid oscillation cycles (<400ms), whereas fatigue yawning maintains sustained high MAR plateaus (>1,500ms).

MAR = ( ||m2 - m8|| + ||m3 - m7|| + ||m4 - m6|| ) / ( 2 * ||m1 - m5|| )
NOMINAL ORAL: < 0.40 SPEECH DYNAMICS: 0.40 - 0.65 (High Frequency) CONFIRMED YAWN: > 0.70 (>1.5s sustained)
ORAL APERTURE LANDMARK TOPOLOGY
PERCLOS P80 Operational Metric

Percentage of Eye Closure over time (P80 criterion). The ISO and NHTSA gold standard in drowsiness detection: tracks the proportion of time within a rolling 60-second window that the eyelids cover 80% or more of the pupil area.

PERCLOS = ( ∑ t_closed[EAR < 0.20] ) / ( T_window[60s] ) * 100%
ALERT: < 7.5% DROWSINESS ONSET: 7.5% - 12.0% SEVERE RISK: > 12.0%
