Multimodal Inspection

Engineered for Cabin Precision

Traditional drowsiness systems rely solely on eye aspect ratio or isolated frame predictions. AeroDMS Sentinel fuses high-frequency facial geometry with deep object detection across temporal windows.

👁️

Ocular Kinetics & Blink Tracking

Computes Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) across 3D sub-pixel landmarks. Distinguishes natural involuntary blinks (80–400 ms) from sustained eye closures and microsleep episodes (>1.2 s).

🥱

Yawn vs. Speech Suppression

Calculates Mouth Aspect Ratio (MAR) with an empirical speech-suppression temporal filter. Requires ≥1.5–2.0s of continuous oral dilation, preventing false triggers during conversation.

📈

Rolling 60s P80 PERCLOS

Accumulates cumulative proportion of time eyes are ≥80% closed within a sliding 60-second window. Serves as the golden standard physiological indicator of oncoming drowsiness.

🧭

3D Head Pose Estimation

Solves Perspective-n-Point (PnP) Euler angles (Yaw, Pitch, Roll) using 3D canonical facial points to detect driver distraction, slumped posture, and prolonged off-road head turns.

⚙️

Continuous Fatigue Index

A unified 0–100 continuous fatigue index synthesized from weighted physiological vectors, providing calibrated warning buffers before severe drowsiness occurs.

🧠

Gated Continual Adaptation

Controlled edge adaptation utilizing a 3-tier label hierarchy and anti-catastrophic-forgetting replay mix, protected by an automated multi-metric validation gate.

System Decoupling

4-Tier Asynchronous Pipeline

Designed for low-power edge hardware. Decouples heavy deep learning inference from high-frequency sub-pixel landmark tracking.

Tier 1: Video Ingestion & Centroid Lock

WebRTC live camera streaming or offline video decoding. Tracks driver face centroid to ensure single-occupant monitoring consistency.

Tier 2: Dual-Stream Feature Extraction

YOLOv5nu (Nano) executed on amortized keyframes (every 3rd frame, ~14 ms) running concurrently with 478-point MediaPipe face mesh (every frame, ~16 ms).

Tier 3: Temporal Multi-Signal Fusion

Zero-latency (<0.4 ms) state machine tracking EAR, MAR, PERCLOS, and head pose. Enforces configured cooldowns (1.5s warning, 3.0s critical) to eliminate repetitive alert fatigue.

Tier 4: Presentation & Edge Adaptation

In-frame OpenCV rendering with dynamic label inversion (y1 < 25) paired with non-blocking continual learning observation buffering.

Zero Identity Retention

Privacy & Data Governance

Engineered from first principles to monitor fatigue without capturing or storing human identity.

🔒 Zero Facial Recognition

The system does not perform biometric face identification, generates no facial embeddings, and cannot determine driver identity. It tracks only geometric facial landmark proportions.

⚡ Volatile In-Memory Processing

Live webcam and video frames are processed strictly in volatile RAM and immediately discarded. No video streams are saved to permanent disk storage or transmitted to remote servers.

📊 Anonymized Telemetry

Logged session telemetry consists entirely of numerical aspect ratios (EAR, MAR, PERCLOS), head orientation angles, timestamps, and categorical state strings.

⚠️ Scientific Limitation & Non-Diagnostic Disclaimer: AeroDMS Sentinel is an applied engineering research prototype and does not constitute a medical diagnostic device, clinical sleep-disorder evaluation, or ASIL-certified automotive safety system. Critical safety alerts recommend taking a safe rest break and do not replace driver vigilance.
